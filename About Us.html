About Us.

OBJECTIVES:

As there is shortage of proper sports grounds, now it has become trend to go on turfs and play for couple of hours without struggling to bring your own equipment’s. As the sports equipment are not the concern of customers as turfs are providing it, same as well online booking system is also essential to make customer experience easy and time saving.

METHODOLOGY ADOPTED:

We propose a system which is reliable and easy to use. This system has user-friendly UI. This system ensures that the user get their desired search results within couple of seconds, instead of searching numbers on google and talking to each turf manager for booking slots and bargaining the rates. Customers who book slots will instantly get message of the booked slot and bill attached to it in the myBookings.
